# Kai indicator artwork and integration marks

`status/*.svg` are copied verbatim from the user's Kai macOS project at
`notchdex/handoff/notch-indicator/svg`. These 26 exports retain the original
28 × 18 canvas, eight-track scene geometry, and neutral silver gradients.
The scene definitions and animation timing originate in Kai's
`IndicatorScenes.swift` and `NotchIndicatorEngine.swift`.

`integrations/*.svg` are copied verbatim from Kai's
`Sources/NotchPrototype/Resources/integrations`. They are Simple Icons SVGs,
CC0; the original attribution is retained in `integrations/ATTRIBUTION.txt`.
The upstream snapshot was downloaded on 2026-10-11; Slack is from 14.0.0 and
LinkedIn from 9.0.0. Brand marks remain the property of their respective owners.
https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons

Existing Code CLI/provider marks remain unchanged. These assets identify
services and statuses without requesting icons from the network.
